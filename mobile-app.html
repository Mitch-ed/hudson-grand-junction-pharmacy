Rx365 App

Mobile App

Manage Your Prescriptions from Anywhere

The Rx365 mobile app is designed to make managing your medications as easy as possible. Whether you're at home, at work, or on the go, you can request refills, check prescription status, and stay organized, all without making a phone call or waiting in line.

The app is free for iPhone and Android and connects directly to your Hudson Grand Junction Pharmacy account. Prefer your computer? You can also request refills online through our Rx365 web portal.

Features

Everything You Need, Built In

The Rx365 app is straightforward and designed with patients in mind, not complexity.

Quick Refills

Request a refill on any of your active prescriptions with just a few taps. No phone call needed.

Your Medication List

View your medications any time in one organized list. Great for keeping track of what you take.

Status Updates

Track your prescription's progress and know when it's ready for pickup. No more guessing.

Phone or Computer

Use the app on your phone, or sign in to our Rx365 web portal from any computer. Your account works in both places.

Easy Transfers

Request a transfer of your prescriptions from another pharmacy to Hudson Grand Junction Pharmacy.

Insurance Cards

Upload and store your insurance cards in the app so they're always on hand when you need them.

Getting Started

Up and Running in Minutes

Getting set up with the Rx365 app is quick and easy. You don't need any technical knowledge, just a smartphone and a few minutes.

1

Download the App

Search for "Rx365" in the App Store or Google Play, or use the download links on this page to find it directly.

2

Connect to Our Pharmacy

Select Hudson Grand Junction Pharmacy and create your account. Your prescriptions will sync automatically.

3

Manage Your Medications

Request refills, check prescription status, and view your medication list, all from the app. It's that simple.

4

Pick Up In Store

Watch for a status update when your prescription is ready. Swing by during store hours and you're all set.

Questions About the App?

We're Happy to Help You Get Set Up

If you have trouble downloading or logging in, just give us a call or stop by. Our team is glad to walk you through it in person.
